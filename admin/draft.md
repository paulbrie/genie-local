# Romania's Informal IT Schools: Who's Really Competing With Școala Informală de IT

The loudest competitor may already be gone.

Codecool — the only school in Romania that marketed "guaranteed employment," the one that ranked #1 for online visibility with 20,000+ monthly visitors, the one everyone benchmarked against — appears to have quietly wound down its Romanian operation. Its local entity, CODECOOL TALENT HUB ROMANIA SRL, reported **0 RON turnover, 0 profit, and 1 employee in 2025**, a −100% year-over-year collapse ([termene.ro](https://termene.ro/firma/41175352-CODECOOL-TALENT-HUB-ROMANIA-SRL)). No formal closure was ever announced. So before mapping the competitive landscape, note the twist: the fiercest-sounding rival to Școala Informală may have removed itself from the board.

That matters, because Codecool played a different game than almost everyone else — and understanding why is the key to the whole market.

## The reference point

Școala Informală de IT was the **first alternative IT school in Romania**, founded in **Cluj-Napoca in 2013**. Today it claims roughly **15,000 graduates** and took "Education Initiative of the Year 2024." It runs online evening and weekend courses across Cluj, Bucharest, Iași, Brașov, Craiova, Sibiu, and Timișoara ([scoalainformala.ro](https://scoalainformala.ro/)).

Its position is deliberately mid-market: about **€1,200 per course** with installments, sitting between free options like freeCodeCamp and premium job-guarantee bootcamps ([pareri-cursuri.ro](https://pareri-cursuri.ro/2026/03/29/scoala-informala-de-it-2026-pareri-pret-15-000-absolventi-merita/)). The catalog is broad — web (JS/React), backend (Java/Python/Node), mobile, QA, data science, UX/UI, DevOps, cybersecurity, business analysis, DBA, RPA — taught by **120+ working practitioners**.

The critical detail: it's **part-time, not a full-time intensive bootcamp**. You keep your day job and learn on the side. That single choice defines both its moat and its exposure.

## Two ways to read the market — and they disagree

There is no single leaderboard. The two most authoritative rankings point at different winners.

**By revenue (2021 registry data)**, Școala Informală ranked **#3** ([recenzii-cursuri.it](https://recenzii-cursuri.it/topul-primelor-10-scoli-de-it-din-romania-dupa-venituri/)):

1. LinkAcademy — €1,932,038
2. Atelierele Ilbah — €1,809,548
3. **Școala Informală de IT — €1,286,361**
4. SDA (Software Development Academy) — €1,229,781
5. WellCode — €892,397
6. Codecool — €694,476
7. IT School — €488,898
8. Wantsome — €406,210
9. Azimut — €310,397
10. Teachbit — €147,117

One caveat worth keeping in mind: LinkAcademy and Ilbah both bundle non-IT courses (design, marketing) into those totals, so their pure-IT revenue is smaller than it looks — the "#1" and "#2" slots flatter them.

**By online visibility (2022 organic search)**, the order scrambles entirely ([clubitc.ro](https://www.clubitc.ro/2022/12/08/topul-celor-mai-populare-scoli-de-programare-din-romania-codecool-conduce-clasamentul-ca-cea-mai-vizibila-scoala-it/)):

1. Codecool — 20,000+ monthly visitors
2. Link Academy — 7,100
3. IT School — 5,200
4. **Școala Informală de IT — 3,600**
5. WellCode — 3,000
6. Info Academy — 2,300

The lesson isn't "which ranking is right." It's that **revenue and attention are decoupled**. Codecool built a content-marketing machine that dominated search while earning a fraction of LinkAcademy's revenue — and, as we now know, without a sustainable business underneath it.

## The real dividing line: pay-per-course vs. pay-from-salary

Strip away the brand names and Romania's informal IT schools fall into two camps.

**Camp one — part-time, pay-per-course.** You pay upfront (or in installments), study around your job, and no one promises you a hire. This is Școala Informală, LinkAcademy, Atelierele Ilbah, and IT School. It's lower-risk for the school and lower-pressure for the student.

**Camp two — full-time intensive with a job guarantee or income-share agreement (ISA).** This was **Codecool's** signature: €0 upfront, two months free, then you pay 15–20% of your salary *after* you're hired — a ~10-month full-time bootcamp claiming ~99% employability ([pareri-cursuri.ro](https://pareri-cursuri.ro/2026/03/28/codecool-romania-2026-pareri-model-isa-platesti-din-salariu-merita/)). Bootcamp-style players like **Jademy** (a Java-only, 16-week intensive), **Code Labs Academy** (500-hour online programs), and **CodersLab** (Polish chain, intensive Python/JS) live in this camp too.

Școala Informală's strength — breadth of catalog, first-mover brand, a large mentor pool, national and online reach — comes with a matching weakness: it does **not** offer Codecool-style job guarantees. In a market where career-changers want certainty, that was the one flank a rival could attack. Codecool did exactly that. And Codecool is the one that seems to have folded.

## The other players worth knowing

- **SDA (Software Development Academy)** — pan-European, teaching across ~6 countries, focused on upskilling and reconversion. Notably, it **merged with Codecool**, tying its fate to that same ISA bet ([sdacademy.ro](https://sdacademy.ro/)).
- **LinkAcademy** — the 2021 revenue leader and former visibility runner-up. An international online provider (students in 110+ countries), 26 programs, all in Romanian, with a free language course and a free repeat-year guarantee. Broad, not IT-only ([link-academy.com](https://www.link-academy.com/)).
- **Atelierele Ilbah** — a wide authorized-course provider spanning design and IT (HTML/CSS/JS, C++, PHP, MySQL, Laravel), claiming 95%+ employment or entrepreneurship ([ateliereleilbah.ro](https://www.ateliereleilbah.ro/cursuri-it/)).
- **IT School** — reconversion-focused, practice-heavy, internationally certified; even launched a "Tech Events" app for Romania ([itschool.ro](https://itschool.ro/)).
- **WellCode** — individualized mentoring and flexible formats; the school most often benchmarked head-to-head against Codecool ([pareri-cursuri.ro](https://pareri-cursuri.ro/2026/03/29/scoala-informala-de-it-2026-pareri-pret-15-000-absolventi-merita/)).
- **Smaller and niche:** NewTech Academy (online part-time tracks, 28–36 weeks), Academia Programatorilor (Bucharest, learn-from-zero), plus Wantsome, Azimut, Teachbit, Info Academy, and StepIT Academy.

## Why the field is this crowded

One number explains the swarm: Romania has cited a shortage of roughly **15,000 programmers**, with Bucharest holding the most IT jobs ([sdacademy.ro](https://sdacademy.ro/blog/cum-sa-alegi-cursul-de-programare/)). A talent gap that large pulls in reconversion schools of every shape — part-time, bootcamp, online, hybrid — all chasing the same career-changers.

In that scrum, Școala Informală's advantages are durable: it got there first, it teaches the widest catalog, and its 120+ practitioner-mentors and national reach are hard to replicate quickly. Its main structural risk was the job-guarantee model it chose not to offer — and the biggest champion of that model just contracted to near-zero in Romania.

## Read the numbers with your eyes open

A few honest caveats, because the tidy rankings above hide some soft spots:

- **The rankings are dated.** Revenue figures are **2021**; visibility figures are **2022**. Current standings have almost certainly shifted.
- **The Codecool wind-down is inferred, not announced.** It rests on one registry aggregator's financials ([termene.ro](https://termene.ro/firma/41175352-CODECOOL-TALENT-HUB-ROMANIA-SRL)). Confirm directly before treating it as settled fact.
- **The headline stats are marketing.** "15,000 graduates," "99% employability," "95%+ employed" are self-reported and unaudited.
- **Many sources are promotional.** Review-aggregator and school-homepage sites shaped much of this picture; the registry-derived revenue numbers are the most objective data point available, and even those mix IT with non-IT courses.

The shape of the market is clear enough: a broad, crowded field split between pay-per-course generalists and pay-from-salary bootcamps, with Școala Informală anchored as a first-mover generalist in the middle. The specifics — who leads, who's shrinking, who's already gone — deserve fresh verification before anyone bets on them.
